## OVERVIEW

We are creating a German-language Barbie-style cartoon ad inspired by the attached reference video. ## AUDIO REQUIREMENTS

### Voice-Over
- **Language:** German
- **Speaker:** Female, warm, natural, conversational — NOT a radio announcer
- **It is a DIALOGUE between multiple characters** — ideally use different voice actresses:
  - Barbie (main): slightly emotional, vulnerable in the beginning → confident by the end
  - Friend off-screen (Scene 1): casual, friendly
  - Doctor: calm, professional
  - Friend at café: confident, warm, been-there-done-that
  - Colleague at office: surprised, curious
- **Pacing:** Conversational, not rushed. Let emotional moments breathe.
- **Audio quality:** Clean, studio-recorded. No echo, no background noise, no compression artifacts.

### Music
- Soft, emotional piano or acoustic guitar in the beginning (problem/struggle scenes)
- Gradually transitions to more uplifting, hopeful music during the café conversation
- Confident, warm music during the transformation/office scenes
- **Volume:** Music sits UNDERNEATH the voice-over at all times — never competes with dialogue
- **Style reference:** Think Pixar emotional moments — subtle, supportive, not overpowering

### Sound Effects (subtle)
- Soft door knock sound (Scene 1)
- Gentle ambient café sounds (Scenes 8–12)
- Soft hair flip swoosh (Scene 13)
- Optional: gentle "ding" or sparkle sound when baby hairs are mentioned

## CRITICAL NOTES FOR THE EDITOR

1. **SCENE-VOICE SYNC:** Every scene change must match the voice-over content. When Barbie talks about "hair in the shower," we SEE her in the bathroom. When she mentions the product name, we see or are about to see the product. Never show a scene that contradicts or is unrelated to what's being said.

2. **HAIR PROGRESSION IS EVERYTHING:** The entire ad's visual story is told through Barbie's hair:
   - Scenes 1–7: THIN, wide part, sparse, sad
   - Scene 13: RECOVERING, baby hairs visible, getting fuller
   - Scenes 14–17: FULL, thick, shiny, worn open and down
   - This progression must be OBVIOUS and visually clear.

3. **PRODUCT PACKAGING:** Use ONLY our provided HaarVita by NATURAPlus™ product image. Do NOT design a new product. The packaging in the video must be identical to our real product.

4. **SUBTITLES:** White text, centered, bottom third. Word-by-word sync with voice-over. Clean, readable font. Subtle drop shadow for readability. Same style as the reference video.

5. **PACING:** Don't rush the emotional moments. The crying scene (Scene 2), the café revelation (Scenes 10–12), and the baby hairs moment (Scene 13) should all breathe. These are the moments that hook the viewer.

6. **NO ENGLISH TEXT** anywhere in the video — subtitles, on-screen text, product labels: everything must be in German.

7. **TOTAL LENGTH:** Aim for 2:00–2:20 minutes. The reference is ~2:18.
